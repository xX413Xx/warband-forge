Keywords Glossary

Trench Crusade — All Keywords from the Digital Rulebook v1.0.2

Effect Keywords

ARMOUR PIERCING Effect
Halve the −INJURY MODIFIER for the target's Armour Characteristic and Armour Battlekit (rounding fractions up in the attacker's favour). Shields unaffected.
ASSAULT Effect
A model can Shoot with this weapon and still Charge or Fight in the same Activation.
AUTOMATIC (X) Effect
When used to Shoot, make X separate Ranged Attacks (each requiring its own Success Roll). All must target the same model.
BLAST (X) Effect
On a hit, all models within X" of the target are also hit (friend and foe). Each hit model gets a separate Injury Roll.
BLOCK Effect
When this model is targeted by a Melee Attack, after the Success Roll, take a Risky Success Roll. If successful, the attack is blocked (no Injury Roll). Each weapon with BLOCK can only block once per Activation.
CLEAVE (X) Effect
When you Fight with this weapon, make X separate Melee Attacks (can target different enemies). Resolve one at a time.
COVER Effect
Model has the Cover or Defended Obstacle attack modifiers.
CRITICAL Effect
On a Critical Success, add +2 INJURY DICE instead of the normal +1.
CUMBERSOME Effect
Requires two hands even if the model has STRONG. Can still be used with a Shield Combo shield.
DANGEROUS TERRAIN Effect
Activating in or moving into this terrain requires a Risky Success Roll. Fail = Injury Roll, activation ends.
DEADLY Effect
Injury Roll uses 3D6 (pick 3 highest/lowest). +/−INJURY DICE still apply but you pick the 3 best/worst.
DEMONIC Effect
Model has the NEGATE FIRE Keyword.
DIFFICULT TERRAIN Effect
Every 1" moved across this terrain counts as 2".
FEAR Effect
−1 DICE to Melee Attacks targeting this model. Models that cause FEAR are immune to FEAR.
FIRE Effect
After the Injury Roll, place 1 extra Blood Marker on target even if No Effect.
FIRETEAM Effect
Part of a pair. Both can activate simultaneously, taking actions in any interleaved order. If either's activation ends, both end.
FLAMETHROWER Effect
Ranged Attack is automatically a Success (no roll). Cannot achieve Critical Success.
FLYING Effect
Measures movement through the air. Must end on battlefield/terrain. Still takes Risky for Dangerous terrain. No Injury Roll from Falls.
GAS Effect
After the Injury Roll, place 1 extra Blood Marker on target even if No Effect.
GOLEM Effect
Out of Action → Down instead (unless Bloodbath). Cannot remove own Blood Markers. Has NEGATE FEAR and NEGATE GAS. Cannot have TOUGH.
HEAVY Effect
Max 1 HEAVY item. No Charge Bonus. If on a Ranged Weapon/Grenade, cannot Shoot and Move/Charge/Retreat/Dash in the same Activation.
HELD Effect
Requires one hand, cannot be put down. Model limited to 1H Weapon or Shield only (no 2H). May still carry Grenades.
IGNORE ARMOUR Effect
Ignore −INJURY DICE and −INJURY MODIFIERS from target's Armour Characteristic, Armour, and Shield.
IGNORE [MODIFIER] Effect
Ignore the specified modifier. E.g. IGNORE COVER means Cover penalty doesn't apply.
IMPASSABLE TERRAIN Effect
Models cannot move onto or across this terrain.
IMPERVIOUS Effect
ARMOUR PIERCING and IGNORE ARMOUR do not affect this Battlekit's −INJURY DICE/MODIFIERS. Other Battlekit is affected normally.
INFILTRATOR Effect
Deploy anywhere on battlefield out of Line of Sight and 8"+ from enemies. Deployed after non-Infiltrators.
LEADER Effect
+1 DICE to Morale Checks if at least 1 friendly LEADER is on the battlefield and not Down/Out of Action.
MINED Effect
Contact triggers detonation (SHRAPNEL Injury Roll). FLYING models only trigger if they end on it. NEGATE MINED = immune.
NEGATE [KEYWORD] Effect
Model is not affected by the specified Keyword's Effect. E.g. NEGATE GAS ignores GAS.
PISTOL Effect
Can be used as Ranged or Melee weapon (or both in same Activation). As Melee, can use Ranged or Melee Characteristic. Can be Off-Hand.
REGENERATE (X) Effect
When activated, before taking any Actions, remove up to X Blood Markers from this model.
RELOAD Effect
After attacking with this weapon, the model's Activation ends.
RISKY Effect
Success Rolls with this Battlekit become Risky Success Rolls (fail = activation ends). No additional effect if already Risky.
SCATTER Effect
If the attack misses, the shot scatters. Roll D6 for direction and D3 for distance in inches from the target point.
SHOTGUN Effect
If the target is within half the weapon's range, add +1 INJURY DICE to the Injury Roll.
SHRAPNEL Effect
After the Injury Roll, place 1 extra Blood Marker on target even if No Effect.
SKIRMISHER Effect
When an enemy declares a Charge against this model, it may immediately make a free Move (up to its Movement Characteristic) before the charge is resolved.
STRONG Effect
Can use 2-Handed Weapons in one hand (except CUMBERSOME). Allows carrying more weapons simultaneously.
TOUGH Effect
First Out of Action result is treated as Down instead. Once Down, can be taken Out of Action normally.

Tag Keywords

ARTIFICIAL Tag
Model is an artificial construct. Interacts with certain faction abilities.
BLACK GRAIL Tag
Member of the Cult of the Black Grail faction.
CONSUMABLE Tag
In campaigns, this Battlekit is lost at the end of a game in which it is used.
DEPLOYABLE Tag
Battlekit represented by a model or terrain piece set up during the game.
ELITE Tag
The most senior and heroic models in a Warband.
HERETIC Tag
Member of the Heretic Legions faction.
INFECTION MARKERS Tag
Model is under the influence of a supernatural or chemical malady. See Warbands of Trench Crusade for full rules.
NEW ANTIOCH Tag
Member of the Principality of New Antioch faction.
PILGRIM Tag
Member of the Trench Pilgrim faction.
THE COURT Tag
Member of the Court of the Seven-Headed Serpent faction.
