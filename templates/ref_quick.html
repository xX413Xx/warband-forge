Quick Reference

Trench Crusade — Core Rules Summary

Turn Sequence

1. Initiative Phase — Count models on the battlefield (not Down or Out of Action). Fewest models = Initiative. Tied? Roll off. Initiative player chooses who activates first. Carry out start-of-turn tasks.

2. Activation Phase — Players alternate activating one model at a time. Each model activates once per turn. If one player runs out, the other activates remaining models one by one.

3. Morale Phase — If half or more of your models are Down or Out of Action, take a Morale Check (Success Roll, +1 DICE if you have a LEADER). Fail two turns in a row = you lose. Then carry out end-of-turn tasks.

Actions (Once Each per Activation)

  • Move — Move up to Movement Characteristic. Cannot move within 1" of enemies (must Charge instead).
  • Charge — Pick visible enemy within 12". Roll D6 + Movement. If within 1" at end, may Fight. Cannot Shoot + Charge same activation (unless ASSAULT).
  • Retreat — Move away from enemy within 1". Must end 1"+ from all enemies. Enemy gets one free Fight before you move.
  • Dash — Additional move (on top of Move/Charge/Retreat). Risky Success Roll first; fail = activation ends.
  • Shoot — Ranged Attack. Must be 1"+ from enemies. Cannot Shoot + Charge/Fight (unless ASSAULT).
  • Fight — Melee Attack against enemy within 1".

Success Table

Roll (2D6)Result
2–6Failure
7–11Success
12+Critical Success (+1 INJURY DICE)

+DICE: add dice, pick 2 highest. −DICE: add dice, pick 2 lowest. Pairs cancel out.

Risky Success Roll: If you fail, the model's activation ends immediately.

Ranged Attack Sequence

  1. Choose Weapon
  2. Pick Target (visible, in range)
  3. Check Line of Sight
  4. Check Range
  5. Determine Modifiers
  6. Take Success Roll
  7. If hit → Injury Roll

Down Rules

  • If taken Down during activation → activation ends
  • −1 DICE to all rolls while Down
  • +1 INJURY DICE for melee attacks vs this model
  • Cannot move while Down (unless Falling)
  • Stands up when next activated; movement halved that activation

Battlekit Limits (per model)

  • 1× 2-Handed Ranged OR 2× 1-Handed Ranged
  • 1× 2-Handed Melee OR 2× 1-Handed Melee
  • 1× Grenade type
  • 1× Armour, 1× Shield
  • Shield limits: 1× 1H Melee + 1× 1H Ranged max (no 2H unless both have Shield Combo)
